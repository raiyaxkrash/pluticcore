    * Unfinished spins safely resume after server reboot or player reconnect.
    * Active sessions strictly removed only after successful outbox commit.

    * Chip tier locked into NBT upon round start to prevent offhand swap exploits.

      preserve the Joker.

      date, and jackpot amount in NBT. Right click displays live server pool.
  - Safe Drop:
    * Split large jackpot payouts into stacks <= 64; cleanly dropped at feet
      if inventory is full without duping or voiding items.
  - Automatic Config Migration:
    * Legacy v1 configs automatically upgraded to v2 balanced defaults without
